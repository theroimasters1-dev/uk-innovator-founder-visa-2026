# UK Innovator Founder Visa 2026: Comprehensive Endorsement & Settlement Blueprint

The **UK Innovator Founder Visa** is the premier immigration pathway designed by the UK Home Office for ambitious entrepreneurs, tech innovators, and startup founders seeking to establish and scale an innovative enterprise in the United Kingdom.

Unlike legacy immigration routes, the Innovator Founder Visa has removed the mandatory £50,000 minimum investment fund requirement, while simultaneously opening an accelerated **3-year pathway to Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR)**.

---

## 🏛️ Core Eligibility Criteria: The 3 Statutory Tests
Every applicant must secure a formal Letter of Endorsement from an authorized assessing body:
1. **Innovation:** Must possess a genuine, original business plan that satisfies new or existing market needs, creating a measurable competitive edge in the UK market.
2. **Viability:** Demonstrated technical ability, commercial acumen, realistic financial cash flow projections, and market validation.
3. **Scalability:** High-growth commercial potential, structured roadmap for employment creation, and domestic economic contribution.

---

## 📋 Authorized Endorsing Bodies (2026)
Applicants cannot apply directly to the Home Office without prior endorsement from one of the official assessing organizations:
- **Envestors Limited**
- **UK Endorsement Services (UKES)**
- **Innovator International**
